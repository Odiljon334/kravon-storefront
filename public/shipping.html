Logistics Information

Shipping & Delivery

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Global Order Fulfillment

Orders placed on KRAVØN are automatically dispatched from our automated logistics hubs (Shenzhen / Yiwu) via premier global linehaul carriers including CJPacket Sensitive Pro, SF Express, and national postal carriers.

2. Delivery Estimates

  • United States: 5 to 11 business days
  • United Kingdom & European Union: 6 to 12 business days
  • Canada & Australia: 7 to 14 business days
  • Rest of the World: 8 to 16 business days

3. Order Processing & Tracking

All acquisitions are processed and packaged within 24 to 48 hours of payment authorization. Once handed over to the freight carrier, a confirmation email containing an international end-to-end tracking link is transmitted to your email.

4. Shipping Rates

Complimentary standard shipping is provided on all orders totaling $200.00 or more. Standard flat-rate delivery on orders under $200.00 is calculated transparently during bag checkout.
