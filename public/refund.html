Consumer Protection

Refund & Return Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to be completely satisfied with your acquisition. If for any reason you are not content with your order, you may request a return or full refund within 30 calendar days of confirmed delivery.

2. Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a standard return:

  • The item must be in its original condition, unworn, unwashed, and undamaged.
  • Original packaging, serial tags, and documentation must accompany the return.
  • Proof of purchase (Order Reference ID or receipt email) must be provided.

3. Damaged or Defective Items

If an item arrives damaged in transit or exhibits a manufacturing flaw, notify us within 7 days of receipt with photos of the defect. We will immediately dispatch a complimentary replacement or issue a 100% full refund including shipping charges.

4. Refund Processing

Once your return is inspected and approved, your refund will be processed back to your original payment method (Visa, Mastercard, or 2Checkout) within 3 to 5 business days.

5. How to Initiate a Return

Contact our customer care desk at support@kravon-archive.com with your Order Reference ID (e.g. #KR-123456) to receive step-by-step return authorization instructions.
