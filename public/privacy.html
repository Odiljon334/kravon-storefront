LEGAL SPEC // PRIVACY & TELEMETRY

Privacy Protocol & Telemetry Directive

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Revision 2.4.0 · Autonomous Archive Compliance

1. Minimal Logistical Telemetry

KRAVØN operates under zero-knowledge architectural principles. When acquiring limited-run garments or prop artifacts, we collect only essential coordinates required to dispatch parcels: legal recipient name, electronic mail, physical destination address, and mobile phone number for courier synchronization.

2. Payment Instrument Tokenization

All credit and debit transactions are processed directly via Tier-1 PCI-DSS certified payment processors (2Checkout / Verifone). Zero payment card numbers or security codes are ever captured, transmitted, or stored in raw text on KRAVØN systems.

3. Zero Advertising Monopolies

We do not deploy invasive third-party ad pixels, cross-site identity trackers, or third-party marketing brokers. Your browsing telemetry and acquisition history are never sold, rented, or commoditized.

4. Automated Telemetry Purge

Logistical telemetry is retained exclusively for 90 days following confirmed courier delivery to ensure return authorizations and warranty claims. Identifying telemetry is purged automatically following warranty expiration.

5. Client Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Clients retain full legal authority to request immediate extraction or permanent deletion of their transaction records. To invoke data erasure, submit your directive directly to our concierge channel at concierge@kravon-archive.com.
