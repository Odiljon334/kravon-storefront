Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you visit KRAVØN or make an acquisition, we collect personal information necessary to fulfill your order and maintain transaction security. This includes your name, billing address, shipping address, payment information (tokenized via 2Checkout/Verifone), email address, and phone number.

2. Use of Collected Data

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Process and route orders to our global fulfillment network.
  • Transmit tracking updates and shipment notifications.
  • Screen orders for potential fraud or unauthorized activity.
  • Comply with applicable financial and consumer protection laws.

3. Payment Security & Encryption

All card payments are securely handled through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant processors (2Checkout / Verifone). KRAVØN never stores or possesses unencrypted full credit or debit card numbers on local infrastructure.

4. Data Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We disclose data solely to essential third-party logistics partners (e.g. CJPacket, DHL, FedEx) and payment settlement networks to complete your acquisition.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR in Europe or CCPA in California), you have the right to request access to, update, or delete your personal data. Contact us at support@kravon-archive.com for inquiries.
