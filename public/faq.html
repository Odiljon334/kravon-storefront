Atelier Inquiries & Specifications

Direct technical answers regarding materials, transit timelines, statutory EU return protocols, and customs duties.

01 What are the international delivery timelines?
All acquisitions are routed via insured priority lines (CJPacket Sensitive Pro / Special Air Express). Typical transit windows: United States & United Kingdom (5–11 business days), European Union (7–14 business days), East & Central Asia (5–10 business days). Real-time telemetry tracking numbers are dispatched upon automated warehouse routing.
02 How are import duties and customs handled?
For United States consignments, shipments are subject to current U.S. Customs and Border Protection regulatory schedules. For European Union deliveries, value-added taxes (VAT) and the upcoming European customs flat fees are processed in strict accordance with the Union Customs Code.
03 What is the atelier return policy?
We provide a comprehensive 30-Day Atelier Satisfaction Guarantee. In compliance with European Union consumer directives, EU residents possess an unconditional 14-day statutory right of withdrawal from the date of artifact receipt. Items must be returned in their original archival condition with all security seals intact.
04 Are the LED masks and tactical props safe for conventions?
Yes. The Cyberpunk Winged Mask operates on low-voltage lithium cells (5V DC USB rechargeable or dual AA depending on variant) with insulated circuitry and breath ventilation channels. The Ninja Warrior Daggers are manufactured from reinforced lightweight polymer alloys with rounded convention-safe blunt edges.
05 What payment systems are accepted?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, and Google Pay via tokenized 256-bit SSL encrypted gateway pipelines. Customer financial credentials are never exposed or retained on our systems.
