## 2. THREE VIRAL VIDEO CONCEPTS (READY TO SHOOT)

### Concept 1: "The Anonymous City Walk" (Cyberpunk LED Mask)
- **Visual Hook (0–3s):** Total darkness. Heavy bass drop. The dual halo LED rings on the mask flare to cyan life in sync with the beat. Person steps into rain-soaked neon street.
- **Body (3–10s):** Quick cuts showing the braided cyber-cables, magnetic snap-on faceplate, and 4 different light modes (strobe, pulse, steady, stealth).
- **CTA (10–14s):** Text on screen: *"Limited batch release. Serialized archival piece. Link in bio."*
- **Why it converts:** Extremely hypnotic contrast on dark mode mobile feeds. Triggers instant *"Where do I buy this?"* comments.

### Concept 2: "Ferrofluid Sound Visualizer" (Desk Artifact)
- **Visual Hook (0–3s):** Speaker starts playing heavy electronic bass. Black magnetic liquid inside glass cylinder spikes into life and dances synchronously with soundwaves.
- **Body (3–10s):** Person puts magnet close to glass; the spikes twist into an impossible alien geometric sphere.
- **CTA (10–14s):** *"Not CGI. Natural physics artifact. Drop 01 now open."*

### Concept 3: "Traditional Ronin meets Tokyo 2077" (Samurai Denim Jacket)
- **Visual Hook (0–3s):** Close up of distressed black raw-edge denim asymmetric seam. Pull back to show oversized streetwear silhouette walking through Kyoto alleyway.
- **Body (3–10s):** Styling transition: from casual hoodie underneath to tactical urban fit check with deep concealed utility pockets.

## 3. THE 30-DAY GO-TO-MARKET TIMELINE

```
Week 1: Physical Verification & Sourcing
├── Order 1 physical unit of Cyberpunk Mask & Denim Jacket to your address
├── Verify exact packaging, LED battery function, and sewing tolerances
└── Create brand social accounts (@kravon.archive on TikTok & Instagram)

Week 2: Content Creation & Influencer Outreach
├── Shoot 12 high-contrast TikTok/Reel clips using physical samples
├── DM 10 micro-influencers (10k-50k followers in techwear/cosplay niches)
└── Offer free sample in exchange for a dedicated review video + custom 10% code

Week 3: Organic Traffic Activation
├── Post 2 videos daily on TikTok and Instagram Reels
├── Monitor analytics: watch time, save rate, and click-throughs to bio link
└── Engage with every single comment within 15 minutes of posting

Week 4: Conversion Optimization & Retargeting
├── Track initial orders in SQLite database
├── Launch low-budget ($10/day) Meta retargeting ad to site visitors
└── Analyze cart drop-offs and refine checkout experience
```
