## 2. METRIC FORMULAS & BREAK-EVEN ANALYSIS

### Core Metrics:
1. **Average Order Value (AOV):** Projected at **$104.50** (Weighted across Mask $118, Jacket $89, Dagger $54, Hoodie $44).
2. **Gross Margin:**
   $$\text{Gross Margin} = \frac{\text{Revenue} - \text{COGS}}{\text{Revenue}} = \frac{118.00 - 24.50}{118.00} = 79.2\%$$
3. **Contribution Margin (Margin after variable fulfillment & gateway fees):**
   $$\text{Contribution Margin} = \frac{118.00 - (24.50 + 14.20 + 7.08 + 5.90)}{118.00} = \frac{66.32}{118.00} = 56.2\%$$
4. **Break-Even CAC (Maximum Ad Spend allowable per order):**
   $$\text{Break-Even CAC} = \text{Contribution Margin in Dollars} = \$66.32$$
   - If ad spend to acquire one customer exceeds **$66.32**, every sale loses real cash.
   - Target allowable CAC for healthy business: **$30.00 – $42.00**.
5. **Break-Even ROAS (Return On Ad Spend):**
   $$\text{Break-Even ROAS} = \frac{\text{AOV}}{\text{Contribution Margin}} = \frac{118.00}{66.32} = \mathbf{1.78\text{x}}$$
   - Paid ad campaigns must achieve at least **1.78x ROAS** to prevent losses.
   - Recommended target ROAS: **2.50x – 3.20x**.

## 3. THREE REALISTIC FINANCIAL SCENARIOS (MONTH 1–3)

### Scenario A: Naive Paid Ads Failure (Typical Beginner Trap)
- Traffic: 100% paid cold Meta/TikTok ads.
- Conversion Rate: 0.9% (Cold international traffic).
- CAC: $74.00 (High ad auction friction).

- AOV: $118.00.
- Result: **Net Profit of +$51.82 per order (43.9% Net Margin)**. Highly profitable.

- AOV: $128.00 (Cross-sells with Dagger set / accessories).
- Result: **Net Profit of +$32.40 per order (25.3% Net Margin)**. Sustainable and scalable.

## 4. TEN CRITICAL BUSINESS MODEL CHECKS

1. **Is $0 startup capital truly possible?**  
   *No.* Minimum liquidity of **$1,500 – $2,500** is required to float CJ fulfillment orders before payment gateway payouts clear, plus sample seeding costs.
2. **Are factory prices ($12–$25) authentic?**  
   *Yes, verified.* CJ Dropshipping and 1688 show authentic factory quotes: $24.50 for LED faceplates, $21.00 for raw-edge denim.
3. **Is $54–$118 competitive?**  
   *Yes.* Equivalent techwear brands (ACRONYM, Fabric of the Universe) sell masks for $180–$280 and jackets for $220–$450. KRAVØN sits at an accessible luxury entry tier.
4. **Is shipping cost accounted for?**  
   *Now modelled.* Free shipping over $200 encourages 2-item baskets, absorbing the $14.20 shipping overhead into a higher gross dollar margin.
5. **Is customs duty a barrier?**  
   *Under $800 de minimis in the US:* Parcels enter the US duty-free under Section 321.  
   *EU / UK:* Subject to VAT (IOSS). Must be clearly stated in checkout.
