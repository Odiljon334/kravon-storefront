## 1. BRAND TONE OF VOICE: THE "CONFIDENT ARCHIVIST"

KRAVØN's voice must strike a precise balance between **understated luxury** and **cutting-edge technical craftsmanship**.

- **Not Hypebeast:** Avoid cheap street slang (*"Cop this crazy drip before it sells out!!!"*).
- **Not NASA Simulation:** Avoid awkward pseudo-scientific robotic phrasing (*"TRANSMITTING DESTINATION TELEMETRY TO ENCRYPTED VAULT"*).
- **The Ideal Tone:** Calm, restrained, precise, and exclusive. Treat every product like a serialized museum artifact engineered for real-world wear.
