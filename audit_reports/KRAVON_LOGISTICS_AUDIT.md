## 1. THE SHIPPING TIME REALITY (HANDLING VS TRANSIT)

Marketing a cross-border Chinese factory item as "24–48h express delivery" is the number one cause of credit card chargebacks in dropshipping.

### Actual Step-by-Step Timetable:
1. **Order Ingestion & Payment Clear:** 0–24 hours (Automated via KRAVØN server).
2. **Factory Domestic Dispatch (Shenzhen/Yiwu to CJ Hub):** 2–3 business days.
3. **CJ Warehouse Quality Inspection & Repacking:** 1–2 business days.
4. **Air Cargo Export Customs & Flight Departure:** 2–3 business days.
5. **Destination Customs Clearance (US / EU Hub):** 1–2 business days.
6. **Last-Mile Courier Delivery (USPS / DHL / Hermes):** 3–5 business days.
- **Total Real-World Door-to-Door Delivery:** **12 to 18 Business Days (16 to 24 Calendar Days)**.

### Policy Disclosure Recommendation:
Update shipping disclosures to set honest, premium expectations:
- *"Each artifact undergoes individual circuit and craftsmanship calibration prior to dispatch (3–4 days). Dispatched via dedicated tracked air freight (8–12 business days)."*
This reframes the wait time as deliberate quality control rather than slow postal transit.

## 2. SPECIAL HAZARD & RESTRICTION AUDIT (BATTERIES & LIQUIDS)

### A. Cyberpunk LED Light-Up Winged Mask (SKU: `KR-CYB-MASK-01`)
- **Hazard:** Contains dual lithium button/rechargeable cells and LED wiring.
- **Air Freight Classification:** UN3481 (Lithium ion batteries contained in equipment).
- **Courier Mandate:** Standard cheap airmail (ePacket / China Post) will reject this parcel. It **MUST** ship via specialized battery lines: **CJPacket Sensitive Pro** or **YunExpress Special Line**.
- **Shipping Cost Impact:** Surcharge of +$3.50 to +$5.00 compared to non-electronic garments.

### B. Ferrofluid / Liquid Magnetic Artifact
- **Hazard:** Contains suspension oil, magnetic nanoparticles, and glass housing.
- **Air Freight Classification:** Liquid cargo (requires leak-proof secondary containment and MSDS certification).
- **Courier Mandate:** Must be shipped via **CJPacket Liquid Line** or sea freight for heavy batches.

## 3. CUSTOMS, VAT & IMPORT TAXES

1. **United States (The E-Commerce Sweet Spot):**
   - **Section 321 De Minimis:** Shipments valued under **$800.00 USD** per recipient per day enter the US completely duty-free and tax-free. All KRAVØN items ($44 to $118) pass directly through US customs with zero tariff friction.
2. **European Union (EU IOSS Rules):**
   - Since July 2021, the EU €22 tax exemption was abolished. All commercial goods imported into the EU are subject to VAT (typically 19%–21%).
   - If KRAVØN does not collect VAT at checkout under an IOSS registration number, European customers will receive a postal notice requiring them to pay 20% VAT + a €6 courier collection fee before delivery.
   - *Fix:* Ensure CJ Dropshipping's consolidated IOSS option is checked in order payloads to pre-clear European VAT.
